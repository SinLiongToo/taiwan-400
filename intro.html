Introducing Taiwan's History如何用英文介紹台灣歷史

What to say when someone asks "So what's Taiwan's history?" A version you can say in 30 seconds, the six eras with a sentence for each, the words people often get wrong, and short answers to the questions that usually follow.

當有人問「台灣的歷史是怎樣的？」時可以怎麼回答：一段 30 秒就能說完的版本、六個時代各一句話、常被用錯的詞彙，以及接下來常被問到的問題與簡短回答。

The Qing era is by far the longest, at 212 years. Japanese rule lasted 50 years and the Republic of China has governed for 80.

清領時期最長，達 212 年；日治 50 年，中華民國至今統治 80 年。

The 30-second version 30 秒版本Read it aloud; it takes about half a minute　照著唸，大約半分鐘

Taiwan was home to Austronesian Indigenous peoples for thousands of years before any state arrived. In the 1600s the Dutch and Spanish set up trading posts, until Koxinga, a Ming loyalist, drove the Dutch out in 1662. The Qing Empire ruled the island for two centuries, then lost it to Japan in 1895. After 1945 the Republic of China took over. The 228 Incident of 1947 and 38 years of martial law followed, and in the 1980s and 90s Taiwan became a democracy. Its people's sense of who they are has kept changing ever since.
在任何國家到來之前，台灣數千年來是南島語族原住民族的家園。1600 年代，荷蘭人與西班牙人在此設立貿易據點，直到明朝遺臣鄭成功於 1662 年逐走荷蘭人。清帝國統治台灣兩百多年，1895 年將台灣割讓給日本。1945 年後由中華民國接管，隨後發生 1947 年的二二八事件與長達 38 年的戒嚴；1980 與 90 年代，台灣走向民主。從那時起，台灣人對「我們是誰」的認同也持續在改變。

Six eras, a sentence for each 六個時代，各一句話Start with the sentence; add the details if there is time　先說那一句，有時間再補充細節

BEFORE 1624 · 1624 年以前

An Indigenous island 原住民族的島嶼

SAY · 這樣說

Before 1624, Taiwan belonged to its Indigenous peoples. Many linguists think the Austronesian languages, spoken from Madagascar to Hawaiʻi, first spread from here.

1624 年以前，台灣屬於島上的原住民族。許多語言學家認為，從馬達加斯加到夏威夷都有人使用的南島語言，最早就是從這裡擴散出去的。

  • Villages governed themselves; in the centre a multi-village alliance, the Middag kingdom, ruled several communities.各部落自治；中部有跨部落的大肚王國，統領多個社群。
  • Chinese fishermen, traders and pirates worked the coast, but the Ming dynasty only patrolled Penghu, the islands in the strait.漢人漁民、海商與海盜在沿海活動，但明朝只在海峽中的澎湖巡防。
1624 – 1662

Dutch and Spanish Formosa 荷西時期

SAY · 這樣說

The Dutch East India Company ran the southwest as a trading colony from Fort Zeelandia, near today's Tainan, while Spain held the north until 1642.

荷蘭東印度公司以今台南附近的熱蘭遮城為據點，把西南部經營成貿易殖民地；西班牙則佔據北部，直到 1642 年。

  • The company traded deerskins and sugar, and brought Chinese settlers over to farm.公司經營鹿皮與蔗糖貿易，並招募漢人來台開墾。
  • It ruled Indigenous villages through elders at yearly assemblies (the Landdag), and put down the Guo Huaiyi uprising of Chinese farmers in 1652.它透過每年的「地方會議」經由長老統治原住民村社，並在 1652 年鎮壓漢人農民的郭懷一事件。

Dutch & Spanish Formosa 荷西時期Guo Huaiyi, 1652 郭懷一事件

1662 – 1683

The Zheng kingdom 明鄭・東寧

SAY · 這樣說

Koxinga (Zheng Chenggong) besieged Fort Zeelandia for nine months and expelled the Dutch, setting up the first Han Chinese state on Taiwan.

鄭成功圍攻熱蘭遮城九個月，逐出荷蘭人，在台灣建立第一個漢人政權。

  • His son Zheng Jing ran it as the Kingdom of Tungning, still using the fallen Ming calendar.其子鄭經以「東寧」為名治理，仍沿用已滅亡的明朝永曆年號。
  • The Qing admiral Shi Lang took Penghu in 1683 and the kingdom surrendered.1683 年清朝水師提督施琅攻下澎湖，東寧政權投降。

Koxinga takes Taiwan 鄭成功攻台

1683 – 1895

Qing rule 清領時期

SAY · 這樣說

For about two centuries the Qing treated Taiwan as a frontier. Settlers from Fujian and Guangdong farmed the western plains while the mountains stayed Indigenous.

大約兩百年間，清朝把台灣當作邊疆。來自福建與廣東的移民開墾西部平原，山區則仍是原住民族的天地。

  • Life was turbulent. The Lin Shuangwen (1787) and Dai Chaochun (1862) rebellions broke out, and settler groups fought feuds by home region.社會動盪：爆發林爽文（1787）與戴潮春（1862）等民變，移民也依祖籍分類械鬥。
  • After the 1874 Mudan Incident the Qing invested more. Taiwan became a province in 1885 and got its first railway in 1891.1874 年牡丹社事件後，清廷加強經營；1885 年台灣建省，1891 年第一條鐵路通車。

Lin Shuangwen 林爽文Dai Chaochun 戴潮春Settler feuds 分類械鬥

1895 – 1945

Japanese rule 日治時期

SAY · 這樣說

China ceded Taiwan to Japan in 1895, and Japan became the first state to govern the whole island, mountains included.

1895 年清朝將台灣割讓給日本，日本成為第一個統治全島（包括山區）的國家。

  • It built railways, schools, police and land registers, and fought resistance from the 1895 war to the Tapani (1915) and Musha (1930) uprisings.日本建立鐵路、學校、警察與土地登記制度，並鎮壓從乙未戰爭到西來庵（1915）、霧社（1930）的各種抵抗。
  • During the war with China it pushed assimilation (the Kōminka movement), and Allied bombing hit the island in 1944–45.中日戰爭期間推行同化政策（皇民化運動）；1944–45 年台灣遭盟軍轟炸。

1945 – TODAY · 至今

The Republic of China on Taiwan 戰後・中華民國

SAY · 這樣說

The Republic of China took over in 1945 and moved its government to Taipei in 1949. After decades of authoritarian rule, Taiwan democratised from the late 1980s.

中華民國在 1945 年接管台灣，1949 年中央政府遷到台北。經過數十年威權統治，台灣從 1980 年代末走向民主化。

  • The 228 Incident (1947) was followed by martial law from 1949 to 1987, a period known as the White Terror.二二八事件（1947）之後，是 1949 到 1987 年的戒嚴，這段時期稱為白色恐怖。
  • Since then Taiwan has had direct presidential elections from 1996, the first change of ruling party in 2000, an apology to Indigenous peoples in 2016, and Asia's first same-sex marriage law in 2019.此後有 1996 年首次總統直選、2000 年首次政黨輪替、2016 年總統向原住民族道歉，以及 2019 年亞洲第一部同婚法律。

English中文What it means · 意思

Names and spellings 人名與地名的拼法Why the same name can be written three ways　同一個名字為什麼有好幾種寫法

Taiwan spent decades using older romanisations, mainly Wade–Giles, before it adopted Hanyu Pinyin in 2009. People and many places keep their established spellings. Use the form a person used for themselves, and expect your listener to know the older one.

台灣數十年來使用較舊的拼音系統（主要是威妥瑪拼音），2009 年才改採漢語拼音。人名和許多地名仍保留原本的拼法。介紹人物時，用當事人自己使用的拼法；聽眾通常也比較熟悉舊拼法。

Saying it carefully 謹慎的說法Habits that keep you accurate and avoid arguments　讓說法準確、少起爭執的習慣

  • Say who governed, and when.

    "The Qing ruled Taiwan from 1683 to 1895" is a fact your listener can check. Words like "always" and "never" turn history into an argument.

    說清楚誰統治、在什麼時候。

    「清朝在 1683 到 1895 年統治台灣」是聽眾可以查證的事實；「一直」「從來不」這類字眼，會把歷史變成爭論。

  • Remember the Qing ruled only part of the island.

    For most of its 212 years Qing government stopped at the foothills. Japan was the first state to control the mountains.

    記得清朝只統治部分地區。

    212 年中的大部分時間，清朝的治理止於山腳；日本才是第一個控制山區的國家。

  • Keep "Taiwan" and "the Republic of China" distinct when it matters.

    Taiwan is the island and its people; the Republic of China is the state that has governed it since 1945. Before 1945 the ROC had never ruled Taiwan.

    必要時區分「台灣」與「中華民國」。

    台灣指這座島和島上的人；中華民國是自 1945 年起統治台灣的國家。1945 年以前，中華民國從未統治過台灣。

  • Give disputed numbers as ranges, with a source.

    For 228, official records confirm at least 8,324–11,841 dead and missing (Chen Yi-shen and Hsueh Hua-yuan, eds., 2021). Older estimates run higher.

    有爭議的數字用範圍表示，並註明出處。

    以二二八為例，官方文獻可確認的死亡與失蹤人數至少 8,324–11,841 人（陳儀深、薛化元主編，2021）；較早的推估更高。

  • Name Indigenous peoples individually.

    "The Seediq rose at Musha in 1930" tells more than "the aborigines rebelled".

    說出原住民族的族名。

    「1930 年賽德克族在霧社起事」比「原住民造反」傳達更多資訊。

  • Leave room for different memories.

    Families experienced Japanese rule, 1945 and martial law in very different ways. "Many people remember…" is often truer than "Taiwanese felt…".

    為不同的記憶留空間。

    不同家庭對日治、1945 年與戒嚴的經驗差異很大。「許多人記得……」往往比「台灣人覺得……」更貼近事實。

Questions people ask 常見問題Short, factual answers　簡短、根據事實的回答

Has Taiwan always been part of China? 台灣一直都是中國的一部分嗎？

It has had several rulers. Before 1624 no state governed it. The Dutch and Spanish came next, then the Zheng kingdom (1662–1683), the Qing (1683–1895), Japan (1895–1945) and, since 1945, the Republic of China. The People's Republic of China claims Taiwan but has never governed it, and Taiwan's international status remains disputed.

台灣經歷過多個統治者。1624 年以前沒有任何國家統治；之後是荷蘭與西班牙，接著是明鄭（1662–1683）、清朝（1683–1895）、日本（1895–1945），1945 年起是中華民國。中華人民共和國主張擁有台灣，但從未統治過台灣；台灣的國際地位至今仍有爭議。

Who are the Taiwanese? 台灣人是誰？

About 23 million people. Most descend from Hoklo or Hakka settlers of the Qing era. Others are families who arrived with the ROC after 1945. There are 16 recognised Indigenous peoples, and a growing number of new immigrants, many from Southeast Asia. Most people today say they identify as Taiwanese.

約 2,300 萬人。多數是清代福佬或客家移民的後代；也有 1945 年後隨中華民國來台的家庭；另有 16 個官方承認的原住民族，以及人數漸增、許多來自東南亞的新住民。如今多數人表示自己認同為台灣人。

What happened in 1947? 1947 年發生了什麼事？

On February 27 a cigarette raid in Taipei turned violent, and the next day protests spread across the island against Chen Yi's administration. In March troops from China crushed them. Thousands were killed, including many local leaders, and for decades the event could not be discussed in public. February 28 is now a national day of remembrance.

2 月 27 日，台北一場查緝私菸演變成流血衝突，隔天抗議陳儀政府的行動擴及全島。3 月，從中國派來的軍隊展開鎮壓，數以千計的人遇害，其中包括許多地方菁英；此後數十年，這件事無法公開討論。如今 2 月 28 日是國定的和平紀念日。

When did Taiwan become a democracy? 台灣什麼時候成為民主國家？

Gradually. Martial law ended in 1987, and the national legislature was fully re-elected in 1991–1992. Presidents have been directly elected since 1996, and power first passed peacefully to the opposition in 2000.

是逐步完成的。1987 年解除戒嚴，1991–1992 年國會全面改選；1996 年起總統由人民直選，2000 年首次和平移轉政權給反對黨。

Why do people speak Mandarin, Taiwanese and Hakka? 為什麼台灣人說華語、台語和客語？

Each layer of history left a language. Indigenous languages are the oldest. Hoklo and Hakka settlers brought their own, and Japanese was the school language until 1945. Mandarin became the national language under the ROC, and since the 1990s the other languages have been revived in schools and media.

每一層歷史都留下了語言。原住民族語最古老；福佬與客家移民帶來各自的語言；1945 年以前學校教日語；中華民國時期華語成為國語；1990 年代起，其他語言在學校與媒體中逐漸復振。

A five-minute talk, step by step 五分鐘簡報的順序An order that works for a class, a meeting or a visitor　適用於課堂、會議或接待訪客

  1. Start with the map (30 s). 從地圖開始（30 秒）

    An island of about 36,000 km² that the Tropic of Cancer runs through, with mountains taller than any in Japan. Geography explains why the mountains stayed Indigenous for so long.

    一座約 36,000 平方公里、北回歸線穿過的島，山比日本任何一座都高。地理說明了山區為何長期是原住民族的領域。

  2. The first people (30 s). 最早的居民（30 秒）

    Austronesian peoples, thousands of years of settlement, and a language family that reached across the Pacific.

    南島民族、數千年的定居歷史，以及遍及太平洋的語系。

  3. Outsiders arrive by sea (45 s). 外來者從海上來（45 秒）

    The Dutch, the Spanish, then Koxinga. Taiwan entered world trade as a stop between Japan, China and Southeast Asia.

    荷蘭人、西班牙人，然後是鄭成功。台灣成為日本、中國與東南亞之間的轉運站，進入世界貿易。

  4. The Qing frontier (45 s). 清朝的邊疆（45 秒）

    Settlers, rebellions and feuds; a province only in 1885.

    移民、民變與械鬥；直到 1885 年才建省。

  5. The Japanese colony (45 s). 日本殖民地（45 秒）

    Modern infrastructure built alongside armed suppression; a generation educated in Japanese.

    現代化建設與武力鎮壓並行；整整一代人接受日語教育。

  6. 1945 to 1987 (60 s). 1945 到 1987 年（60 秒）

    The ROC takeover, 228, the arrival of the central government in 1949, and martial law.

    中華民國接管、二二八、1949 年中央政府遷台與戒嚴。

  7. Democracy and identity (45 s). 民主與認同（45 秒）

    Elections, apologies and the revival of local languages. Finish with a question, such as how people in Taiwan might tell this story differently.

    選舉、道歉與本土語言復振。最後以一個問題收尾，例如：台灣不同的人會怎麼講這段故事？
